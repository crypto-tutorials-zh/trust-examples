## Safety Feature Comparison

### Security Standards
- **Cold Storage Requirement**: Binance, Coinbase, OKX (80-90% reserves)
- **2FA Support**: All major exchanges (mandatory recommended)
- **Insurance/Fund**: Coinbase ($250k FDIC coverage), OKX (emergency insurance fund), Binance (safu fund)
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Coinbase (SEC/FINRA), Kraken (FinCEN), OKX (Isle of Man license)

### Recommended for:
- **Absolute Beginners**: Coinbase (regulatory safety, simple UI)
- **Cost-Conscious Traders**: Binance or OKX (lowest fees)
- **Regulated Markets (US/EU)**: Coinbase, Kraken
- **Advanced Users**: Binance, OKX (derivatives, API)
- **Non-KYC Entry**: Huobi, OKX (P2P trading options)

## Funding Methods by Region

### China-based Users
- P2P trading (Huobi, OKX): popular for USDT/BTC entry
- International card: limited (Binance, OKX via third parties)
- Crypto-to-crypto: recommended route

### US/EU Users
- Bank transfer: Coinbase, Kraken (direct)
- Debit/Credit card: All (with fees ~3-5%)
- ACH: Kraken (lowest cost)

### Asia-Pacific
- Local payment methods: Binance, Huobi (enabled per country)
- Peer-to-peer: OKX, Huobi (most liquid)

## Emerging Threats & Mitigation

- **Exchange Collapse Risk**: Use hardware wallet; withdraw regularly
- **Regulatory Crackdown**: Prioritize regulated exchanges (Coinbase, Kraken)
- **Phishing**: Enable whitelisting on withdrawal addresses
- **API Key Theft**: Use read-only keys; rotate quarterly

## Decision Tree for New Users

```
1. Do you live in the US or EU?
   → YES: Choose Coinbase (safest) or Kraken (better fees)
   → NO: Continue to step 2

2. Do you need local fiat on-ramps?
   → YES: Binance, OKX, or Huobi (check local availability)
   → NO: Continue to step 3

3. Priority: low fees or maximum safety?
   → FEES: OKX or Binance
   → SAFETY: Coinbase (regulated) → Kraken (audited) → Binance

4. Advanced trading (futures, options)?
   → YES: Binance or OKX
   → NO: Coinbase or Kraken
```
